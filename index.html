3DGS Workstation

3DGS Workstation is a personal workstation environment for photogrammetry and 3D Gaussian Splatting.

It uses COLMAP, LichtFeld Studio and Spirula Studio for 3D reconstruction.

Google Drive Integration

Google Drive is used to transfer and store input images, videos and generated 3D models through rclone.

This application is intended for personal use and is not offered as a public service.
