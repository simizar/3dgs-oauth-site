Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Purpose

3DGS Workstation is a personal tool for photogrammetry and 3D Gaussian Splatting.

Google Drive Access

The application uses rclone to access Google Drive with the user's authorization. It may list, read, upload, modify and delete files as directed by the user.

Data Storage

OAuth credentials are stored in a private persistent storage volume. Files may be processed locally or on a cloud GPU server.

Data Sharing

Google Drive data is used for personal 3D processing workflows. It is not sold or shared for advertising purposes.

Revoking Access

Users can revoke access through their Google Account security settings.
